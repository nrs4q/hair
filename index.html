About the Film

Hair is a psychological Southern Gothic arthouse horror film focused on atmosphere, character, symbolism, and emotional realism rather than spectacle. The story explores themes of loneliness, secrecy, aging, memory, fixation, and the hidden rituals individuals create to cope with isolation.


The project contains no explicit sexuality, no nudity, no graphic gore, no political agenda, no anti-religious messaging, and no extreme violence. The emphasis is psychological tension, mood, and emotional resonance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why this film? +
Hair was developed from a deeply personal screenplay and years of artistic exploration. The project seeks to create meaningful, emotionally resonant cinema through atmosphere, visual symbolism, and character-driven storytelling.
What makes the project unique? +
The film is being developed entirely outside traditional studio systems. It embraces practical locations, handcrafted production elements, local collaborators, and a highly personal creative vision.
What audience is the film intended for? +
Audiences who appreciate independent cinema, psychological horror, Southern Gothic storytelling, arthouse filmmaking, literary themes, and visually atmospheric narratives.
Is this a commercial horror film? +
No. While the film contains horror elements, its primary focus is psychological atmosphere, emotional depth, symbolism, and character.
Where is the film being produced? +
Production is based in Shelbyville, Tennessee and developed through a network of local artists, creatives, filmmakers, and collaborators throughout Middle Tennessee.
What is the long-term vision? +
Blood Brothers aims to create thoughtful independent cinema that explores film, literature, psychology, philosophy, memory, and visual symbolism through deeply personal storytelling.

About Blood Brothers

Blood Brothers is a grassroots independent film project created by a small group of brothers and longtime friends from rural Tennessee. The collective exists to create meaningful art through collaboration, friendship, craftsmanship, and storytelling.


Rather than pursuing spectacle or controversy, the project focuses on emotional realism, atmosphere, literature, psychology, philosophy, memory, and visual symbolism. Every aspect of production embraces a handmade approach rooted in local talent and authentic creative passion.
